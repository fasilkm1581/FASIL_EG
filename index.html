More about us

Emirates Gold is a refinery and mint based in Dubai with a history going back 30 years of manufacturing and trading precious metals locally and internationally. Built over several decades from humble beginnings to a full-service refinery and mint, with a strong customer focus and an emphasis on ethical and responsible sourcing principles, our reputation is second to none.

Our Services

What we do

From scrap to investment-grade bullion, every step is done under one roof in Dubai.

Scroll
01

Refining

Refining precious metals such as scrap gold and silver, scrap jewellery, or even higher grade gold doré requires a process that eliminates unwanted materials and purifies the output. This task plays a pivotal role in the production of investment grade gold and silver bars.

Learn More
02

Assaying

Our laboratory handles gold assaying, metal analysis, and research & development. Assaying monitors quality throughout refining, determining fineness of gold and silver, with traditional fire assay as well as spectrometry.

Learn More
03

Smelting

Our smelting department melts fine metal into bars or produces grains from scrap metal for use in our refinery. It is a critical step in almost all of our services and central to our daily operations.

Learn More
04

Bullion Trading

We produce a wide range of gold bullion bars refined up to 999.9, with purities matching market demand. All bars are inspected and weighed with precision before being packaged and dispatched.

Learn More
05

Minting Coins & Bars

Our Die Making & Minting division is equipped with state-of-the-art machinery to produce coins, medals and customised products, with an extensive collection of decorative motifs.

Learn More
06

Precious Stones Separation

We separate diamonds and precious stones from gold and silver in finished jewellery, a cost-effective way of recycling old jewellery and stock, carried out by experienced and patient chemists.

Learn More

Corporate Gifting

Corporate gifts are an excellent way to nurture connections. A gesture of gratitude keeps your company at the top of everyone's mind and reminds clients and employees that you care.
